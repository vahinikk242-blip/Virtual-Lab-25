Introduction to Cheminformatics

The concepts behind the lab, in four short ideas.

⚗️

What is cheminformatics?

Cheminformatics combines chemistry ⚗️, computer science 💻 and data analysis 📊. It uses computational techniques to store, process, visualize and analyze chemical information.

🔤

SMILES

Simplified Molecular Input Line Entry System represents a molecular structure as a text string.

EthanolCCO
Benzenec1ccccc1
Acetic AcidCC(=O)O
📐

Molecular descriptors

Descriptors give numerical information about a structure.

  • Molecular Weight
  • LogP
  • TPSA
  • Hydrogen Bond Donors
  • Hydrogen Bond Acceptors
  • Rotatable Bonds
🧊

3D molecular models

Different models help students understand molecular geometry.

  • 🟢 Ball and Stick: atoms as spheres, bonds as sticks
  • 🟡 Stick: emphasizes chemical bonding
  • 🔵 Space Filling: approximate molecular volume
  • ⚫ Wireframe: simplified representation

SMILES cheat sheet

Atoms

Write the symbol: C, N, O, S, P, F, Cl, Br, I. Hydrogens are implied.

Bonds

Single is implied. Use = for double and # for triple.

Branches

Parentheses attach a side chain to the preceding atom, as in CC(C)O.

Rings and aromatic atoms

Matching digits close a ring. Lowercase letters mark aromatic atoms, as in c1ccccc1.
